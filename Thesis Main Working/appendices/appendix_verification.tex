\chapter{Verification Snapshot}

This appendix records the targeted verification work carried out while constructing the thesis workspace. The purpose is not to claim comprehensive software assurance. The purpose is to document that the most thesis-critical pathways were executed directly and observed to pass.

\section{Interpretation of the Verification Checks}

The verification commands cover three different risk classes. Pricing and reporting tests protect the thesis against silent errors in the localized reward and artifact-writing layers. Crop-planning and hierarchical tests protect the semantics of the environments that the thesis discusses in Chapters~3 and~4. The document rebuild check protects the writing pipeline itself by confirming that the thesis PDF can be regenerated from versioned assets and bibliography files.

\section{Residual Verification Gaps}

These checks do not replace the full fresh experiment campaign, and they do not prove agronomic validity. They are engineering checks on the correctness of code pathways and artifact generation. The main empirical risk remains the completion and audit of the latest broad NPK matrix, which is why the results chapter is still staged as provisional.
